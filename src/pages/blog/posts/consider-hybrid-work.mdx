Fashion and Accessories :Folding fans are sometimes used as stylish accessories, especially in vintage fashion circles or for special events like weddings and parties.

Performing Arts: They are used in certain performing arts, such as dance and theater, particularly in performances that incorporate elements from different cultures.

Cultural Celebrations: During cultural festivals or events , folding fans are often featured and appreciated.

Practical Use: In hot weather, some people do use folding fans for practical purposes to stay cool.

Collectibles and Gifts: Folding fans are also popular as collectibles and as decorative items. They can be found in gift shops, especially those that focus on cultural or handmade items.
